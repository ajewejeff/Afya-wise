Prevention

1. Malaria Prevention

Malaria is an illness spread by infected mosquitoes. It can cause fever, chills and headache.

How to prevent malaria

  • Sleep under an insecticide-treated mosquito net.
  • Remove stagnant water where mosquitoes breed.
  • Seek prompt testing if you develop a fever.

Do not rely on symptoms alone or self-medicate. Seek urgent care for severe symptoms.

Nutrition

2. Healthy Eating

Healthy food provides energy, supports growth and helps your body work properly.

Eat a variety of foods

  • Eat vegetables and fruits regularly.
  • Include beans, eggs, fish or other protein foods.
  • Choose staple foods such as ugali, rice, potatoes or millet.
  • Drink water and limit sugary drinks and excess salt.
🧼
Hygiene

3. Handwashing

Handwashing with soap helps prevent the spread of germs and infections.

The correct method

  • Wet hands with clean water and apply soap.
  • Rub all parts of your hands for at least 20 seconds.
  • Rinse and dry with a clean method.

Wash before eating and after using the toilet.

🧠
Wellbeing

4. Mental Health

Mental health affects our thoughts, feelings, relationships and ability to manage stress.

Care for your mind

  • Sleep regularly and take study breaks.
  • Talk to a trusted person when you feel overwhelmed.
  • Exercise and spend time with supportive people.

If distress persists or disrupts daily life, consider professional support.

❤️
Disease prevention

5. Blood Pressure and Diabetes

These conditions can cause serious health problems and may not have obvious early symptoms.

Reduce your risk

  • Be physically active.
  • Eat balanced meals and reduce excess salt.
  • Avoid tobacco and seek health checks when recommended.

Ask a healthcare professional about your personal risk and appropriate screening.

💧
Daily habits

6. Water and Sleep

Water supports body functions. Sleep helps learning, mood, recovery and concentration.

Healthy habits

  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Most adults need at least seven hours of sleep each night.
  • Keep a regular bedtime and reduce late-night caffeine.

Move your body

Fitness and Sports Guidance

Choose safe activities that you enjoy and increase activity gradually.

🏃

Move for better health

Most adults should aim for 150–300 minutes of moderate physical activity per week. Some activity is better than none.

  • Try walking, dancing, cycling, swimming or football.
  • Warm up before exercise.
  • Drink water and rest when needed.

Daily Fitness Tip

Start with a comfortable 10-minute walk.

Stop exercising and seek urgent medical care if you experience chest pain, fainting or severe difficulty breathing.

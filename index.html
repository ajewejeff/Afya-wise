Malaria Prevention

Malaria is spread through bites from infected mosquitoes.

How to prevent malaria

  • Sleep under an insecticide-treated mosquito net.
  • Remove standing water around your home where safe.
  • Seek medical assessment if you develop fever.

Do not self-diagnose. Testing helps identify the cause of fever.

🧼

Personal Hygiene

Good hygiene helps reduce the spread of germs.

Healthy hygiene habits

  • Wash hands with soap before eating.
  • Wash hands after using the toilet.
  • Brush your teeth regularly.
  • Use safe water and keep your surroundings clean.
💧

Safe Water and Food

Unsafe food and water can spread infections.

Protect yourself

  • Drink water from a safe source.
  • Cook food thoroughly.
  • Keep cooked food covered.
  • Wash fruits and vegetables using safe water.
🫁

Respiratory Health

Learn ways to reduce the spread of respiratory infections.

Protect your breathing health

  • Cover coughs and sneezes.
  • Keep indoor spaces well ventilated.
  • Clean your hands regularly.
  • Seek urgent help for severe breathing difficulty.
🥗

Healthy Nutrition

A varied diet helps your body get the nutrients it needs.

Eat a variety of foods

  • Eat vegetables and fruits regularly.
  • Include beans, eggs, fish or other protein foods.
  • Choose staple foods such as ugali, rice or potatoes.
  • Drink safe water and limit sugary drinks.
🧠

Mental Wellness

Your mental wellbeing is an important part of your health.

Care for your mind

  • Keep a regular sleep routine.
  • Take breaks when studying.
  • Talk to a trusted person when stressed.
  • Seek professional support when you need it.

🏃 Fitness and Sports Health

Beginner Exercises

  • Start with a comfortable walk.
  • Try gentle bodyweight exercises.
  • Increase activity gradually.

Prevent Sports Injuries

  • Warm up before activity.
  • Use suitable shoes and equipment.
  • Rest and stop if you feel sharp pain or dizziness.

Hydration and Rest

Drink safe water, eat balanced meals and allow your body enough time to recover.
